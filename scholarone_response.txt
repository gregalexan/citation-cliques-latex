Original title: Citation Cliques in Low Impact Journals
Revised title: Citation-Network Cohesion and Reciprocal Citation Cliques Across Journal-Impact Groups: A Matched Author Analysis

Dear Dr. Larivière,

Thank you for inviting a major revision of manuscript QSS-2026-0077. We also thank Reviewer 1 for the detailed and constructive assessment. We have rebuilt the affected analysis and substantially revised the manuscript. The new title reflects the associational matched-author design and names the formally defined reciprocal-clique analysis without implying intent.

The principal revisions are as follows:

1. We reorganized the manuscript so that the data, matching procedure, graph definitions, paired inference, and anomaly screen are introduced before the results. Technical terms, notation, analytic populations, denominators, and missing-value rules are now defined in plain language as well as formally.

2. We replaced the deterministic 10% ORCID sample with every eligible non-empty ORCID in the fixed snapshot. We retained the inclusive hard caliper |h5,Case - h5,Control| <= 3 and the deterministic greedy order: subject, absolute h5 difference, Case ORCID, and Control ORCID. A read-only audit reproduces the generated full-cohort match and checks positive h5, no caliper violations, and no reused author-subject keys.

3. We replaced the former metric table with self-contained operational definitions. The former “normalised burst intensity” variable and its Kleinberg attribution were removed. The revised maximum annual dyadic surge share is explicitly defined as a bounded five-year scalar summary, not a trajectory model or an implementation of Kleinberg’s burst-detection method.

4. We removed K-means clustering and the former three-cluster result. We also removed feature weights, the four-sigma cutoff, and the Cohesion Composite Score. The revised anomaly screen uses five declared structural features, Control-referenced median/IQR scaling, Control percentile cutoffs, and sensitivity analyses across thresholds, seeds, and leave-one-feature-out fits. The overlap between the Isolation Forest score and the cohesion restriction is quantified and described as an overlapping guardrail, not independent confirmation.

5. We now specify two-sided paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, outcome-specific complete-pair deletion, within-family Benjamini–Hochberg correction, matched rank-biserial effects, paired-bootstrap intervals, and sign-flip checks. The rebuilt analysis reports eight outcomes in two declared families. We removed both the earlier “citation trajectories” claim and the inconsistent 13-versus-14 / 12-out-of-14 feature claims.

6. We removed the unsupported hub-and-spoke characterization, Louvain analysis, modularity result, qualitative topology labels, and the fixed-candidate graph-rewiring comparison. A clique is now a maximal clique of at least four nodes in the positive undirected projection, with density and weighted reciprocity formally defined and thresholded (primary rule: density >= 0.75, reciprocity >= 0.50). We convert overlapping qualifying cliques to one membership value per author-subject and compare Case and Control membership within the original matched pairs, conditioning on discordant pairs. Primary-rule membership was 10.96% for Cases and 3.82% for Controls (paired difference 7.14 percentage points; exact p < 0.001), with sensitivity reported across minimum size and reciprocity thresholds. The revision also separates the fixed journal-level Eigenfactor exposure from the author-level directed weighted graph and its undirected projection, stating exactly where direction and weights are retained or discarded.

7. We rewrote the Discussion to compare the regenerated findings with the relevant author- and journal-level literature, explain why the measures are not numerically interchangeable with prior studies, and discuss non-adversarial explanations. The conclusion now states explicitly that the study is associational and that the screen cannot establish coordination, intent, or misconduct.

8. All reported values, tables, and figures now come from one canonical full-cohort analysis output. The verified v4.0.0 replication archive is deposited on Zenodo.

We provide a clean revised manuscript, a marked manuscript with deletions shown in small red text and additions in bold, and a detailed point-by-point response. Page and line references in the detailed response refer to the clean revised manuscript.
